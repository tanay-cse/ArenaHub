Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about Arena Hub rules, hardware, and reservations.

Log in with your registered student account, click on "Book a station", choose your desired station (PC VIP, PS5, Racing Sim), and select an available 1-hour slot starting on the hour. You'll receive instant confirmation.

Every registered player starts with a base rating of 1200. When you play against another registered member, either player can submit the match result via "Record match". The system computes new ELO ratings using standard competitive tournament math.

You automatically earn points for completing bookings and winning competitive matches. Check the "Rewards" store to redeem your accumulated points for free station time, snacks, and exclusive arena merchandise.

All students with an active Arena Hub account can browse open tournaments under the "Tournaments" tab and register before slots fill up. Brackets and match schedules are updated in real time.
